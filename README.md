# Folder Locker

Folder Locker helps you keep your personal files organized and protected in a private vault on your Android phone.

## How It Works

- **Secure Vault:** Import files into Folder Locker to keep them in its private storage.
- **Folders:** Create folders and subfolders to organize your files.
- **File Management:** Rename, copy, move, open, share, export, or delete files.
- **File Categories:** Browse your images, videos, music, and documents in separate sections.
- **Biometric Security:** Use your password and enable fingerprint or supported phone security options for convenient access.
- **Media and Games:** Enjoy the app's built-in media and games sections.
- **Community:** Register or log in to share posts, like, comment, and reply to other users.

## Your Files

You can import files into your vault and export them back to your phone when needed. Keep your login details private and remember your password.
